# Monthly cost estimate: JEV for article-to-dataset classification

I couldn't open the linked page, so this uses the pricing you gave: **$0.042 per million input tokens, output free**. Token counts are estimates, because JEV's tokenizer may differ from the common ~4 characters/token English rule of thumb.

## 1. Unit pricing

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Input price | $0.042 / 1M tokens |
| Per 1,000 tokens | $0.000042 |
| Per token | $0.000000042 (4.2 × 10⁻⁸) |
| Output price | $0 |

## 2. Tokens per article

- 6,000 characters ÷ ~4 chars/token ≈ **1,500 tokens**
- Cross-check: 900–1,000 words ÷ 0.75 words/token ≈ 1,200–1,330 tokens, so 1,500 is a slightly conservative baseline.

Per-article cost at 1,500 tokens: 1,500 × $0.000000042 = **$0.000063** (about 6 hundredths of a cent). That works out to about **$0.063 per 1,000 articles**, or roughly **15,900 articles per $1**.

## 3. Volume

- 5,000 articles/week
- Monthly: 5,000 × 52 / 12 ≈ **21,667 articles/month**

## 4. Cost by scenario

| Scenario | Tokens/article | Weekly tokens | Weekly cost | Monthly tokens | **Monthly cost** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Low (lean text, ~1,300 tokens) | 1,300 | 6.5M | $0.27 | 28.2M | **$1.18** |
| **Baseline (article only)** | 1,500 | 7.5M | $0.32 | 32.5M | **$1.37** |
| Article + dataset definition/labels (~250 tokens) | 1,750 | 8.75M | $0.37 | 37.9M | **$1.59** |
| High (messy HTML, non-English, long prompt) | 2,500 | 12.5M | $0.53 | 54.2M | **$2.28** |

The annual cost is about **$14–$27**, with about **$16.40** at baseline.

## 5. Output tokens

Output is free, so the label, the confidence score, and any other options such as "evidence", "not relevant" or "other dataset" add **$0**. Cost depends only on the text you send in.

## 6. Sensitivity to multiple datasets

If each article is checked against several datasets (for example seizures of different products), cost scales linearly with the number of checks, unless the datasets can be evaluated in one call:

| Datasets per article | Monthly cost (baseline, 1,500 tokens) | Monthly cost (1,750 tokens) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | $1.37 | $1.59 |
| 5 | $6.83 | $7.96 |
| 10 | $13.65 | $15.93 |
| 50 | $68.25 | $79.63 |

## 7. Summary

- **Cost per article:** about $0.00006–$0.0001
- **Weekly cost:** about $0.27–$0.53
- **Monthly cost:** about **$1.20–$2.30**, with **~$1.40–$1.60 as the realistic planning figure**
- **Break-even intuition:** even at 50 datasets, the cost stays under about $80/month.

## 8. Not included

- Crawling and monitoring infrastructure, storage and orchestration, which will likely cost far more than inference.
- Human review of borderline cases.
- Retries, re-processing of updated articles, or duplicate articles. Deduplicating before classification can cut cost further.
- Any minimum fees or rate limits on JEV's side. Check the pricing page for these.
