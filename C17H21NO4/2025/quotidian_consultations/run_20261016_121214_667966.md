## 4. Sensitivity

The realistic range is **about $1.20 to $2.30 per month**.

## 5. Scaling factors

- **Multiple datasets:** if each article is scored against N datasets and you resend the full text each time, cost scales linearly. At 10 datasets the base case is about $15.50 per month, and at 50 datasets about $77. If JEV can score several labels or datasets in one call, it stays near $1.55.
- **Volume growth:** doubling to 10,000 articles per week costs about $3.10 per month. Even 1 million articles per month costs only about $71.
- **Pre-filtering:** dropping obviously irrelevant items by keyword or source before calling JEV would cut cost proportionally, but at this price it is likely not worth the engineering effort.
- **Long articles:** a 3x longer article (18,000 characters) triples its cost, which is still under $5 per month at your volume.

## 6. Costs outside JEV's token price

These will probably exceed the JEV bill:

- Crawling, scraping and article text extraction (cleaning boilerplate also reduces tokens)
- Storage and database costs
- Human review of borderline or low-confidence items, which is usually the largest cost in an evidence-tagging workflow
- Any minimum fees, rate limits or fair-use terms on the "free" output. Confirm these on the pricing page.
- Engineering and monitoring time

## Bottom line

At 5,000 articles per week, JEV costs about **$1.55 per month** (range $1.18–$2.28), roughly **$0.00007 per article**, with about **36.8 million input tokens per month** and no output charges. The token price is negligible next to the other costs in the pipeline. If you can share a few real articles and the exact request format, I can recompute with a measured token count.
