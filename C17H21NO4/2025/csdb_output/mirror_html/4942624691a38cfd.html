    NDLEA intercepts China-bound businesswoman with large consignment of cocaine

    •Pins down N12.3 billion Loud at Lagos port, busts syndicate that plants illicit drugs in passengers’ luggage at motor parks

    A 38-year-old businesswoman, Iwebema Ogechi Peace, has been arrested by operatives of the National Drug Law Enforcement Agency (NDLEA) following the discovery of a large consignment of cocaine concealed in false bottom of her luggage while heading to Beijing, China, aboard a Qatar Airways flight through the Murtala Muhammed International Airport (MMIA) Ikeja Lagos.

    The Director, Media and Advocacy, NDLEA Headquarters, Abuja, Femi Babafemi, said Iwebema, who claims she travels to China to buy items for sale in Nigeria, was arrested on June 21, this year, at the Departure Hall of Terminal 2 of the Lagos airport based on credible intelligence.

    According to the statement, a search of her luggage led to the discovery of four large parcels of cocaine in a false bottom created in her bag. The parcels of the class A drug found hidden in the bag have a combined weight of 7.5 kilograms.

    Babafemi said in another major interdiction, efforts by operatives of two Special Operations Units of NDLEA tracking and monitoring a container laden with 195 big sacks of Canadian Loud, a strong strain of cannabis from Toronto, Canada, for over four weeks paid off on June 23, this year, when a total of 4,959 kilogrammes of the illicit drug consignment worth N12, 397,500,000 in street value, were recovered from the container during a joint examination of the shipment by officers of the Apapa Strategic Command of the agency, men of Customs Service, other security agencies and ports stakeholders at the Apapa Ports Complex, Lagos.

    Babafemi said the large illicit drug consignment, which was loaded into a 40-ft container comprising a Ford and a Nissan vehicle came under NDLEA tracking and monitoring since April 25, this year, when it arrived Toronto, Canada via truck, Montreal via rail on April 29, this year, Tanger Med Morocco on May 11, loaded on another vessel on May 23 before arriving Tin Can Port, Lagos on June 4 and discharged there June 5 before the container eventually left Tin Can and arrived Apapa port on June 22.

    Also, the NDLEA operatives in Abuja, in a joint operation with their colleagues in Anambra State unravelled a syndicate which plants illicit drugs in the luggage of unsuspecting passengers in motor parks. They were intercepted in Abuja with a consignment of methamphetamine in a Sienna bus coming from Nnewi, Anambra State at Abaji, on June 20.

    The methamphetamine was concealed in a black nylon bag, which was also put into another sack of clothes with the phone number of the receiver on it.

    The same day the supposed receiver of the waybill, Mrs. Gloria Peter, was arrested at Utako Motor Park. She, however, denied knowledge and ownership of the package in her luggage. But one of the loaders of the Sienna bus in Nnewi revealed that the drug package was put into the woman’s bag by him on the directive of the driver of the bus Abdurrazak Isah.

    The driver mentioned one of his passengers, Onyebuchi Victor Okoye, as the owner of the drug. Onyebuchi was picked up at Utako and seized an illicit consignment weighed 467.7grams.

    Again, operatives of a Special Operations Unit of NDLEA last Saturday arrested 57-year-old Ikechukwu Uwakwe at a motor park in Iddo, Lagos with 209.50kg Scottish Loud, a strong strain of cannabis being taken to Enugu.

    Two nationals of Benin Republic: Hossou Tito Julien, 30; and Mancellim Dossou, 19 as well as a Nigerian Jackson ThankGod were arrested by NDLEA operatives on the Mile 2 bridge when their truck conveying illicit drug consignment from Togo through Benin Republic was intercepted on June 21.

    The truck had 558,900 pills of Tramadol 250mg, concealed in a fabricated compartment of the vehicle. Two suspects: Lucky Abonga and Osas Azamobo were also arrested on Lagos-Ibadan Expressway last Friday, while transporting 118kg skunk among other legitimate goods in a truck, which left Ebute Ero area of Lagos Island, heading to Onitsha, Anambra State.

    In Lagos, Helen Ese Idiji, 40, and Rashidat Sa’adullah, 53, Babafemi added, were arrested the same day at 15 Olumokun Street, Amukoko, with 28.8 kilograms of skunk recovered from them while the principal suspect, Helen, it was learnt, stores her illicit consignments with the second suspect, Rashidat, from where she distributes to her customers.

    While commending the officers and men of the MMIA, SOUs, Apapa, FCT, Anambra, and Lagos Commands of the agency for the arrests and seizures, Chairman/Chief Executive Officer of NDLEA, Brig.-Gen. Mohamed Buba Marwa (rtd) noted their drug supply reduction efforts.
