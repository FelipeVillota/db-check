LDEA Seizes Over US$20 Million Worth of Drugs in One Year

New DawnJune 29, 2026
1 minute read
Facebook X LinkedIn Tumblr Pinterest Reddit VKontakte Odnoklassniki Pocket Flipboard Skype Messenger Messenger WhatsApp Telegram Viber Line Share via Email Print

By Kruah Thompson

Monrovia, June 29, 2026 – The Liberia Drug Enforcement Agency (LDEA) says it seized 1,419.34 kilograms of illicit drugs with an estimated street value of more than US$20.8 million between July 2025 and June 2026.

The figures were disclosed by LDEA Officer-in-Charge DCP Fitzgerald S. Biango during the observance of the 2026 International Day Against Drug Abuse and Illicit Trafficking at the Paynesville Town Hall.

According to the agency, the seizures included 2,353.396 kilograms of marijuana, 4,263.03 grams of cocaine, 5,880.6 grams of heroin, 15,065.44 grams of kush, 337,246 pills of Tapentadol, 413.7 grams of drug precursors, and 2.384 kilograms of methamphetamine.

The LDEA said Grand Bassa County recorded the highest cocaine seizure at 1,514 grams, followed by Grand Cape Mount County with 1,380.5 grams and Sinoe County with 725.5 grams.

For heroin, the largest quantities were confiscated in Sinoe County (5,278.3 grams), Grand Kru County (555.7 grams), and Maryland County (46.6 grams).

The agency also reported that the highest quantities of kush were seized in Sinoe County (10,964.3 grams), Grand Cape Mount County (1,380.5 grams), and Grand Bassa County (714.9 grams).

Tapentadol seizures were highest in Grand Cape Mount County with 200,139 pills, followed by Lofa County with 85,054 pills and Nimba County with 23,025 pills.

Meanwhile, methamphetamine seizures totaled 2.384 kilograms, including 1.5 kilograms confiscated in Nimba County and 0.884 kilograms in Grand Cape Mount County.

Biango disclosed that between January and June 2026, the LDEA secured the conviction of eight drug traffickers, who received prison sentences ranging from one to 10 years.

He also provided a breakdown of arrests by nationality during the reporting period.

According to the agency, 596 Liberians were arrested, including 464 men and 132 women. The LDEA also arrested 38 Nigerians, 27 Sierra Leoneans, 162 Guineans, 208 Ivorians, and two British nationals.

Overall, the agency reported the arrest of 1,831 suspected drug offenders during the period, comprising 1,800 males and 31 females.

Biango said the LDEA remains committed to strengthening drug enforcement operations while expanding prevention, rehabilitation, and public awareness initiatives across the country.
