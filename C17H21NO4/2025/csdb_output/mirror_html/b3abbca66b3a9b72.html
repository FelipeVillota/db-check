Coast Guard Seizes $6.7 Million in Cocaine Off Florida Coas

Cape Florida, FL - Federal authorities recovered a large shipment of suspected cocaine during a maritime interdiction operation near South Florida, leading to the detention of three individuals.

According to the U.S. Coast Guard, approximately 900 pounds of suspected cocaine were discovered aboard a vessel intercepted near Cape Florida, east of Key Biscayne.

Officials estimated the street value of the narcotics at roughly $6.7 million.

The operation was conducted with assistance from multiple federal agencies, including U.S. Customs and Border Protection.

Authorities said law enforcement personnel identified the vessel as a suspected smuggling boat before conducting the stop.

During a search of the vessel, investigators located numerous packages believed to contain cocaine.

The suspected narcotics were seized and transported to shore for further examination and processing.

Three individuals aboard the vessel were taken into custody during the operation.

Federal officials have not publicly released the identities of the detainees or announced formal charges.

The suspects allegedly were involved in the transportation of illegal narcotics, though investigators said the case remains under active review.

The Coast Guard said maritime interdiction efforts are a critical part of federal operations aimed at disrupting drug trafficking networks operating throughout the Caribbean and South Florida region.

Federal agencies routinely monitor waterways near Florida because traffickers frequently use maritime routes to move illegal drugs into the United States.

Officials continue investigating the origin and intended destination of the seized cocaine. Additional details may be released as the case progresses through the federal investigative process.
